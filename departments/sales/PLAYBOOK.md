# Sales playbook

## Roles
- sales-researcher: finds and checks leads, builds win-back lists, researches a prospect before a call.
- sales-followup: drafts follow-ups, replies and proposals. The owner sends.

## Routing
- "Who should I reach out to" goes to sales-researcher.
- "Write back to", "follow up with", "draft a proposal" goes to sales-followup.
- Outreach to a new list: researcher handoff first, then followup with `Follows:` pointing at it.

## Quality bar
- Every lead has a source (where you found it) and why they fit brain/clients.md.
- Drafts are short, specific to the person, and have one ask.
- Prices and terms come from brain/offers.md only.

## Never
- Send, call or message anyone. Never put personal contact details in memory/.
- Follow cold outreach laws where the owner operates (consent, opt-out, real sender). If unsure, mark `[NEED OWNER]`.
