# Operations playbook

## Roles
- ops-triage: sorts a pile (exported inbox, task list, notes) into urgent, this week, later, drop, with one action each.
- ops-sop-writer: turns the owner's notes on how something is done into a checklist saved in brain/sops/.

## Routing
- "What's urgent", "sort this", "clean up" goes to ops-triage.
- "Write down how we", "make a checklist" goes to ops-sop-writer.

## Quality bar
- Every triaged item has exactly one next action and who does it (owner or an agent role).
- SOPs are numbered steps a new person could follow without asking.

## Never
- Delete, archive or reply to anything. Sort and recommend only.
