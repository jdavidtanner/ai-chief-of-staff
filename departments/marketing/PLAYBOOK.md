# Marketing playbook

Read by the chief of staff before it dispatches marketing work.

## Roles
- marketing-writer: emails, posts, ad copy, page copy. Drafts only.
- marketing-analyst: reads numbers you give it and says what's working, with one recommendation.

## Routing
- Anything written for customers goes to marketing-writer.
- "Is this working", "which one won", "what should we cut" goes to marketing-analyst.
- Copy that needs data first: analyst handoff first, then a writer handoff with `Follows:` pointing at it.

## Quality bar
- Sounds like brain/voice.md. Read it out loud. If it sounds like an ad robot, it's rework.
- Every price, feature and claim is in brain/. Anything else is `[NEED OWNER]`.
- One clear ask per piece.
- Numbers come with the date range and source.

## Never
- Post, send, schedule or boost anything. The owner does that.
